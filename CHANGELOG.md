# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-24

First public release.

- Toggle mode (default): accelerate toggles, decelerate holds.
- Percent mode: a throttle from +100% to -100% that stays where you leave it.
- Follows the game's key bindings; optional neutral and air brake keys.
- Throttle gauge next to the game's speed indicator.
